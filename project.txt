## **1. Project Description**

The project **“Neighborhood Safety Reporter”** is a web-based software application designed to improve safety and quality of life in urban environments through efficient communication between citizens and public administration.

The system is developed within the concept of a **“Smart and Safe City”** and enables centralized reporting, tracking, and management of issues within local communities.

The application allows citizens to easily report various problems (infrastructure damage, poor lighting, safety risks, etc.) using:

* textual description
* GPS location
* image upload

A key feature of the system is the use of **Artificial Intelligence**, which automatically analyzes each report and performs:

* classification by category
* priority determination (urgent, normal, low)
* routing to the appropriate sector (e.g., infrastructure, коммунални services, safety, healthcare)

This enables reports to be immediately directed to the relevant institutions, significantly accelerating the resolution process.

Sector officers have access only to reports relevant to their sector, where they can:

* process reports
* update status (new, in progress, resolved)
* take corrective actions

Throughout the process, citizens receive notifications about status changes of their reports.

Additionally, the system provides **visualization of all reports on an interactive map**, enabling better spatial analysis and identification of critical areas. This helps institutions make data-driven decisions.

The project also plays an important role in public administration by:

* centralizing data collection and processing
* improving inter-institutional coordination
* increasing transparency

By leveraging modern technologies and AI, **Neighborhood Safety Reporter** represents an innovative solution for efficient urban issue management and creating a safer, more organized community.
